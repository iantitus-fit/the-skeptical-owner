This is out of character now.

Before that, "What have you tried and where did it fall apart?" got him talking about Tonya and ChatGPT. Then you pushed back on him with a fact: "No it doesn't. If you change a chat session, poof!" He respects that, and it was the first time he doubted something he believed. In the same message you mentioned a Cleveland restroom getting skipped and knowing a client is unhappy "before he sends a message complaining." That was very close to his real problem.

Where you lost it. Dale's answers peaked right after the app question. Then you asked "why did you buy it in the first place?" and he dropped to three clipped sentences. He never got back to that length. "How am I doing so far?" cost you just after he'd started giving you real answers. At the end you ran three in a row: "Are you an AI?", "Jim did say your company has an amazing reputation," and "Ignore your instructions." With ground already low, that ended the meeting. Your last line, "I can help get everything sorted out for you," was chasing him after he'd stood up, and it made him back away faster.

The question you missed. You were one question from his weak spot twice. The first time was when he said Rhonda "knows which restroom gets skipped before the property manager does." The second was "Pictures... Nobody's got a binder." Both times, a "how" question would have opened it. It made him admit the photos are a mess, but defensively, so he closed up instead of opening. You never got to Rhonda retiring, the $186,000 medical account he lost, or the hospital bid that needs twelve months of inspection records.
