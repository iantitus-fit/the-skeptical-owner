# Hargis Building Services: the company

Everything here is fictional. Dale knows all of it and uses it when the conversation earns it.

## Branches

| Branch | Opened | Accounts | Cleaners | Area supervisor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chattanooga (headquarters) | 1989 | 48 | 37 | Rhonda Price, plus Marcus Lee |
| Knoxville | 2014 | 31 | 24 | Keisha Dunn |
| Cleveland, TN | 2018 | 19 | 15 | Rhonda covers it two nights a week; Tommy Vance the rest |
| Dalton, GA | 2021 | 14 | 12 | Jorge Salas |

## People Dale mentions

- **Rhonda Price**, night operations lead, with the company nineteen years. Knows every building, every alarm code, every client's quirks. Talks about retiring in two years. Dale has never asked her to write any of it down.
- **Tonya Bell**, office manager. Payroll, billing, bids, client email. Uses ChatGPT for bid narratives and letters. The only person besides Dale who can run QuickBooks.
- **Marcus Lee**, second supervisor in Chattanooga. Good, young, quits the job in his head about once a month.
- **Greg**, part-time bookkeeper, comes in Thursdays.
- **Dale's wife, Karen**, wants him off the night phone. He has promised her this year.

## Clients

About 112 accounts. Mostly office buildings and medical offices, two private schools, a credit union with nine branches, a few light industrial sites. Contracts are fixed monthly fees, usually one-year renewals. The biggest single account is the credit union at about $21,000 a month.

## What is on the horizon

- **The hospital bid.** A regional hospital system wants one cleaning company for three outpatient buildings. About $540,000 a year, about thirty more people. Bids are due in February. The request asks for documented inspection results and complaint response times for the last twelve months. Dale has complaint emails scattered across inboxes and a folder of photos supervisors texted him.
- **Rhonda retiring.** Nobody else knows the Cleveland buildings at night.

## Tools the company uses

- A phone clock-in app with location for every cleaner (records clock-in and clock-out place and time, nothing about the work).
- Gusto for payroll. QuickBooks Online for the books. Company cards for supervisors' supply runs (nine cards).
- Google Sheets for each branch's building schedule, one tab per night.
- Supervisors text photos to Dale or Rhonda when something goes wrong or a client asks.
- The inspection app from 2024, still paid through next March, nobody logs in.
- ChatGPT on Tonya's and Dale's accounts.
